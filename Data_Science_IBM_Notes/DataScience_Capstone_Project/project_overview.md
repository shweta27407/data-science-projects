# 🚀 Project Scenario and Overview

The **commercial space age** is here, and multiple companies are making **space travel affordable** for everyone.

## 🌌 Key Industry Players

- **Virgin Galactic** – Offers **suborbital spaceflights**
- **Rocket Lab** – Specializes in **small satellite launches**
- **Blue Origin** – Builds **reusable sub-orbital and orbital rockets**
- **SpaceX** – Perhaps the most successful of them all

---

## 🌟 SpaceX's Notable Achievements

- Sending **spacecraft to the International Space Station**
- Launching **Starlink**, a **satellite internet constellation**
- Conducting **manned missions** to space

---

## 💸 Cost Advantage of SpaceX

- **Falcon 9** rocket launch cost: **$62 million**
- Competitor launches: **$165+ million**
- Key cost-saving factor: **Reusable first stage**

> 💡 *If we can determine whether the first stage lands successfully, we can estimate the cost of the launch.*

---

## 🧰 Falcon 9 Overview

To understand the **scale and function** of the Falcon 9 rocket:

- The **payload** is enclosed in the **fairings**
- **Stage two** helps carry the payload to orbit
- **Stage one** (first stage):
  - Does **most of the work**
  - Is **much larger** and **more expensive**
  - Can be **recovered** and **reused**
