# Expert Viewpoints: Paths in Data Science

In this video, data science professionals share their career journeys and give an overview of the wide range of opportunities in the industry.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaways
- Data science careers often stem from various backgrounds—computer science, public health, engineering, consulting, mathematics, and more.
- Roles evolve over time—many professionals transition from technical to hybrid or business-focused roles.
- Common skills across paths include data storytelling, stakeholder collaboration, statistical analysis, machine learning, and business acumen.

---

## 👤 Career Story 1: Internships → Apple → Coinbase
- **Starting Point**: Internship at **IBM** – hosted a big data hackathon in Beijing.
- Realized data science blends **technical** and **non-technical** skills.
- Completed a degree in **Computer Science** from **Queen’s University**.
- Landed first job as **Data Scientist** at **Apple** (referral from a mentor).

### Role at Apple:
- Supported **Apple TV+ launch**.
- Worked closely with **business and marketing teams**.
- Focused on **data-driven decision making**.

### Next Role: App Store Team at Apple
- Worked in **Product Data Science**.
- Focused on **search and personalization**.
- Collaborated with **engineers** and **product managers**.

### Current Role: **Coinbase**
- Supports **Save and Spend** features (debit card + peer-to-peer).
- Combines **business and product data science** responsibilities.

## 👤 Career Story 2: Consulting → Reporting & Analytics
- **Starting Point**: Consulting for **Fortune 500** and **public sector** clients.
- Began as **Business Systems Analyst** but gradually leaned into **Data Engineering** (BI Warehouses).
- Clients wanted **insights** and **data stories** rather than raw infrastructure.

### Pivot to Reporting and Forecasting:
- Built reports and forecasts for **financials of the Navy**.
- Focused on building **better programs** to support organizational goals.
- Actively engaged with **cross-functional stakeholders** to gather requirements.

## 👤 Career Story 3: IBM Developer → Deep Learning Expert
- **Starting Point**: **WebSphere Developer** at **IBM** (2008).
- Transitioned to **Information Management** roles:
  - DP II Admin
  - DataStage & QualityStage Admin
  - Focused on **data quality** and **integration**

### Key Milestones:
- **2012**: Managed first **Hadoop-based Data Lake** project for a bank in Switzerland.
- Completed a **Master's in Streaming Analytics**.
- Progressed into **Machine Learning**, **Deep Learning**, and now **Federated Learning**.
- Currently specializes in:
  - **Scaling deep learning training**
  - **Distributed systems for AI**

---

## 👤 Career Story 4: Mathematics → Biostatistics → Public Health → Data Science
- **Starting Point**: **Professor** of Mathematics in India (with a degree in **Applied Mathematics**).
- Earned a **Master of Public Health (MPH)**.
- Became a **Biostatistician** and **Epidemiologist** for the **City of Minneapolis**.

### Data Science Transition:
- Moved into data-focused roles:
  - **Data Scientist**
  - **Senior Data Scientist**
  - **Principal Data Scientist**
- Expertise spans **academic research**, **health analytics**, and **technical leadership**.

---

## 🚀 Summary
### Common Themes Across All Paths:
- **Multidisciplinary backgrounds** enrich data science roles.
- Career evolution often involves **pivoting** toward areas of interest (e.g., analytics, engineering, business).
- **Mentorship**, **referrals**, and **continuous learning** play a critical role.
- Data science supports **business**, **product**, and **technical innovations**.

---

## 💡 Advice for Aspiring Data Scientists
- Leverage your unique background—whether it's math, public health, consulting, or engineering.
- Explore both **technical** (e.g., machine learning, big data) and **business-oriented** roles.
- Build soft skills like **communication**, **storytelling**, and **collaboration**.
- Stay open to pivoting and growing across different domains.
