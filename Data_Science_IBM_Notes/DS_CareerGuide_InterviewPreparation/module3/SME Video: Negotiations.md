# Expert Viewpoints: Negotiations  

Negotiating a job offer can feel intimidating, but it’s a **critical step** to ensure your needs and priorities are met. In this video, experts share strategies on **when and how to negotiate effectively**.  

---

## ✍️ Step 1: Define Your Non-Negotiables  
- Before applying, write down what’s **most important to you** and what you will not compromise on.  
  Examples:  
  - Salary range  
  - Work location / remote flexibility  
  - Working hours / work-life balance  
  - Career progression and growth opportunities  
  - Type of projects or work environment  

This clarity helps guide your discussions with recruiters and hiring managers.  

---

## 🤝 Step 2: Have Conversations Early  
- Bring up your non-negotiables with **recruiters, hiring managers, or interviewers**.  
- By the time you receive an offer, you should already know where the company stands on most of these.  
- Negotiation at the offer stage should be about **fine-tuning trade-offs**, not discovering deal-breakers.  

---

## 💡 Step 3: Build a Convincing Case  
When negotiating (e.g., asking for a higher salary):  
1. **Express excitement** about the role first.  
2. **Acknowledge the offer**: “I’d love to take this position, but the salary feels a little low.”  
3. **Present evidence**:  
   - Highlight your skills and contributions.  
   - Compare to market salary data.  
   - Mention your current compensation and why you expect more.  
4. **Counteroffer with confidence**: Request **10–15% more** than your current salary or above their first offer.  

---

## 📊 Step 4: Research & Benchmark  
- Look up salary ranges for your role, location, and seniority using resources like:  
  - Glassdoor  
  - Levels.fyi  
  - LinkedIn Salary  
- Decide on:  
  - **Your target number** (ideal salary).  
  - **Your minimum acceptable number** (bottom line).  
- Counter slightly above your target, knowing they may negotiate down.  

---

## 🏡 Step 5: Negotiate Beyond Salary  
Salary is not the only negotiable factor. Consider:  
- Remote work flexibility  
- Training, certifications, or education budget  
- Performance bonuses  
- Vacation days or paid time off  
- Project responsibilities / role definition  

👉 Don’t compromise too much on what matters to you long term, since you’ll likely stay with the company for years.  

---

## 🎯 Step 6: Tactical Advice  
- **Let the company make the first offer** whenever possible. This sets a baseline for your counter.  
- Stay professional, polite, and collaborative — **you’re not fighting, you’re aligning**.  
- Don’t undervalue yourself: companies expect negotiation.  
- Be prepared with **data, reasoning, and confidence**.  

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways  
- Define **non-negotiables** before you start applying.  
- Use research to know your **market value**.  
- Let the company make the first offer, then counter strategically.  
- Negotiate not only on salary but also on **flexibility, benefits, and growth opportunities**.  
- Approach negotiations with confidence — you are bringing value to the company.
