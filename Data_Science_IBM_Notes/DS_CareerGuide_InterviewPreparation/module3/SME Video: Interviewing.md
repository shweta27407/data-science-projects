# Expert Viewpoints: Interviewing

In this video, experts share **how to be persuasive in an interview**, handle **behavioral questions**, and ask the **right questions** as a candidate.  

---

## 🎯 Persuasive Interviewing
- Show **seriousness**, **positivity**, and **professional body language**.  
- Express **passion for the role** (e.g., why you want to be a Data Scientist).  
- Communicate clearly what you **bring to the table** and how your contributions will benefit the team and organization.  
- Position yourself as someone who will **add value and fit well into the company culture**.

---

## 🧩 Behavioral Interview Questions
Behavioral questions test how you **respond to challenges** and whether there are any **red flags**.  

### Common Behavioral Questions
- *Tell me about a time you set a goal and failed. How did you handle it?*  
- *Describe a conflict with a colleague and how you resolved it.*  
- *Walk me through a recent project you completed.*  
- *What would you do if you were stuck on a problem?*  

👉 **Key Tip**: Interviewers don’t expect perfection. They want honesty, clarity, and lessons learned.  

---

## 🌟 STAR Method for Behavioral Answers
Use the **STAR framework** to structure strong responses:  
- **S (Situation):** Context or challenge you faced.  
- **T (Task):** Your responsibility or goal.  
- **A (Action):** Steps you took to address the issue.  
- **R (Result):** Outcome of your actions (preferably measurable).  

Example:  
*"I led a project where team conflict delayed progress (S). My task was to resolve the conflict and keep the project on track (T). I organized a mediation meeting, clarified responsibilities, and set milestones (A). As a result, we completed the project two weeks early (R)."*

---

## 💡 How to Prepare for Behavioral Questions
- Prepare **stories in advance** for common scenarios (conflict, failure, leadership, teamwork, innovation).  
- **Rehearse** with friends, colleagues, or in front of a mirror to refine your delivery.  
- Stay **authentic** and truthful — don’t try to fake answers.  
- If nervous, practice until your answers feel **natural and confident**.  

---

## 🙋 Candidate Questions to Ask
Smart, thoughtful questions show engagement and preparation. Examples:  
- *What does success look like in the first 6 months of this role?*  
- *Why did the previous person leave this position?*  
- *What does a typical day look like for this role?*  
- *How does the team typically collaborate on projects?*  

---

## 🚫 Questions to Avoid
Avoid questions that send the wrong message or seem self-serving:  
- Company sick leave or vacation policies.  
- Benefits-focused questions in the first interview.  
- Confrontational or overly personal questions.  
- Anything that suggests you didn’t research the company (*e.g., “What does your company do?”*).  

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways
- **Be persuasive** by showing passion, confidence, and relevance to the role.  
- **Expect behavioral questions** and prepare with the **STAR method**.  
- **Ask smart, role-focused questions** that highlight your interest in the position.  
- Avoid questions that focus on **benefits or time off too early** in the process.
