# Expert Viewpoints: Technical Screen Expectations

**Goal of the video:** Understand what to expect during **technical screens** in data science interviews, how they are structured, and the best ways to prepare and perform.

---

## What Are Technical Screens?
- A **technical screen** is an early or mid-stage interview step.  
- It may include:
  - **Coding exercises** (Python, SQL, data structures, algorithms).  
  - **Statistics & probability questions.**  
  - **Experimentation / A/B testing design.**  
  - **Data modeling / ETL design.**  
  - **Product or analytical case studies.**  
- Can be:
  - **Execution-based**: running code, analyzing datasets, building models.  
  - **Conceptual**: reasoning through logic, math, or approach without coding.  

---

## Typical Tasks You Might Encounter
- Query a dataset and return specific insights (SQL, Pandas).  
- Run exploratory analysis to find patterns or hidden information.  
- Implement or **improve an algorithm** for performance.  
- Answer **mathematical or probability-based questions** (more common in junior roles).  
- Tackle **architectural or high-level design problems** (common in senior roles).  
- Apply **product sense**: how data informs product decisions.  
- Discuss **ETL pipelines, scalability, and modeling trade-offs.**

---

## Key Evaluation Criteria
1. **Technical Competence**
   - Coding fluency in **Python & SQL**.  
   - Knowledge of **ML fundamentals** and statistics.  
   - Awareness of **computational efficiency** (time/space trade-offs).

2. **Problem-Solving Process**
   - Do you **clarify the problem** before solving?  
   - Can you structure your approach logically?  
   - Do you identify assumptions and edge cases?  

3. **Communication**
   - Think out loud — let the interviewer follow your reasoning.  
   - Explain **what you’re doing and why** at each step.  
   - Summarize your final approach clearly.  

---

## How to Prepare
- **LeetCode** → practice coding problems, data structures, algorithms.  
- **Kaggle** → practice end-to-end data projects and competitions.  
- **IBM & other online courses** → strengthen Python, SQL, ML integration.  
- Review **probability, statistics, and ML concepts**.  
- Practice **pseudo-coding** and explaining logic step by step.  

---

## Best Practices During the Screen
- **Follow a process:**
  1. Clarify the problem.  
  2. Outline assumptions.  
  3. Define inputs/outputs.  
  4. Decide on functions/operations.  
  5. Walk through your approach step by step.  

- **Think out loud:**  
  - Helps the interviewer see your reasoning.  
  - Lets them guide you with hints if needed.  

- **Communicate trade-offs:**  
  - Why you chose one method over another.  
  - How you’d handle anomalies or edge cases.  

- **Reflect and refine:**  
  - If you notice an error while summarizing, acknowledge it.  
  - Showing correction is better than ignoring mistakes.  

---

## Key Takeaways
- Technical screens are not always perfect mirrors of day-to-day work but are **tests of fundamentals and reasoning ability**.  
- They assess **coding ability, statistics knowledge, analytical reasoning, and communication**.  
- Preparation should combine **practice platforms** (LeetCode, Kaggle) with **core concept review**.  
- Success requires **structured problem solving, clear communication, and adaptability**.
