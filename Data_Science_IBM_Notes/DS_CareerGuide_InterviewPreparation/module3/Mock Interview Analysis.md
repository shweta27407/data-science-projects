# Post-Interview Reflections  

## Antonio’s Feedback (Interviewer)  
I felt that the interview went quite well. Cindy came well prepared from a **technical standpoint**, and her **communication style** was excellent—clear, warm, and welcoming. That style of communication is very important because it shows she’d be a pleasant person to work with, which is always a big consideration for me as a hiring manager.  

From a **technical knowledge** perspective, she got most of the questions right. There were a couple she wasn’t too familiar with, or her answers were a bit shaky—for example:  

> **Question:** “What is the implication for cost on training data?”  
> **Cindy’s response:** “I’m not entirely sure.”  

And that’s fine. We never expect candidates to know all the answers. Nobody does.  

Overall, I was quite impressed. Even before the second interview, I can already say:  
1. There will be a second interview.  
2. We are very likely to hire her.  

---

## Cindy’s Reflection (Candidate)  
I think the interview went pretty well. I don’t want to sound too proud, but I felt I prepared well, and the questions were **fair**—they covered a range of topics in machine learning and data science that aligned with what I reviewed.  

That said, there were a couple of questions I didn’t answer as well as I hoped. Maybe it was nerves, or maybe I hadn’t reviewed that specific topic before the interview. Still, Antonio was very compassionate. He reassured me, saying things like, *“It’s okay if you don’t know this.”* That helped reduce the pressure.  

---

## Michelle’s Reflection (Candidate)  
In my case, I felt my **resume was well tailored** for the role. I showcased experiences and skills that matched the job description closely. My cover letter was also professional and well phrased.  

I think this showed my eagerness for the role and demonstrated that I had the right skills to be a good fit.  

The interview itself went well. Antonio was very **friendly and inviting**. I had prepared thoroughly, so there were questions I knew how to answer. Even when I didn’t know the answer, Antonio guided the discussion in a supportive way.  

Most importantly, the **questions related directly to the job description**, which made it feel relevant and fair.

## Key Takeaways  
- **Antonio:** Impressed with Cindy’s preparation, communication, and technical skills. Confident about moving forward.  
- **Cindy:** Felt well prepared overall, appreciated the fairness of the questions and the supportive tone.  
- **Michelle:** Presented a strong resume and cover letter, felt the interview was relevant, friendly, and aligned with her preparation.
