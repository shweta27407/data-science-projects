# Expert Viewpoints: Data Scientist Roles and Required Skills

In this video, data science professionals discuss:
- Various roles available in data science
- Key technical and soft skills required
- Career advice for aspiring data scientists

---

## 🎯 Common Career Paths in Data Science

### 🧑‍💻 Typical Role Progression
- Associate Data Scientist → Data Scientist → Senior Data Scientist → Principal Data Scientist → Senior Principal Data Scientist (technical track)

### 🏢 Types of Work Environments

#### **Large Companies**
- Teams are larger and cross-functional.
- Collaboration requires structured processes (e.g., **Agile**: sprints, backlogs, planning).
- Greater access to infrastructure and resources.

#### **Small Companies**
- Fewer team members—data scientists may take on broader roles.
- Senior or principal data scientists may mentor others.
- Limited resources—need to **bootstrap** and solve problems creatively.

#### **Independent Contractor / Consultant**
- External expert who supports companies in:
  - Building ML/AI models
  - Solving business problems
  - Providing strategic insights

## 🔄 Related Roles in Data Science

Data science skills are embedded across multiple job titles:

- **Product Analyst**
- **Product Manager**
- **Product Data Scientist**
- **Decision Scientist**
- **Analytics Engineer**
- **Machine Learning Engineer**
- **AI Engineer**

These roles may **not carry the title "Data Scientist"** but are **data science-heavy** and require similar foundational skills.

---

## 🧠 Key Skills Based on Role Type

### 1. **Mathematics & Modeling**
- Strong understanding of **statistics**, **probability**, and **data modeling**.
- Especially valuable for model builders and research-focused roles.

### 2. **Data Integration & Engineering**
- Skills: **SQL**, **ETL tools**, **data quality**, **pipelines**
- Understand business context of the data (e.g., finance, life sciences).

### 3. **Operations & Deployment**
- Familiarity with:
  - **Hybrid cloud environments**
  - **Containers**: Docker, Kubernetes, OpenShift
- Model deployment, monitoring, and scalability

## 💼 Advice for Aspiring Data Scientists

### Step-by-Step Guidance

#### Step 1: Define Your Focus
- Ask yourself: *What type of data do I want to work with?*
- Choose a domain (e.g., marketing, finance, healthcare) and align your learning.

#### Step 2: Build a Portfolio
- Solve real-world problems.
- Document projects as **case studies** that showcase your:
  - Business impact
  - ML/AI skills
  - Data storytelling

#### Step 3: Learn, Write, Share
- **Read** new content
- **Write** about what you learn (blogs, LinkedIn posts)
- **Share** with the community to gain visibility and receive feedback

#### Step 4: Demonstrate Impact
- On your **resume**, show:
  - Time saved
  - Revenue growth
  - Accuracy improvements
  - Business outcomes (quantitative + qualitative)

## 🧩 Suggested Entry Path

> “Start as a **Data Analyst** to gain domain experience, then grow into a Data Scientist role by learning additional skills.”

**Combination of Skills for Transition**:
- Data analysis + data visualization tools
- Programming with SQL and Python
- Applied statistics and research methods

## 🔚 Conclusion

Becoming a data scientist involves:
- Choosing a specialization
- Building a hands-on portfolio
- Developing both technical and domain-specific expertise
- Learning continuously and sharing knowledge
