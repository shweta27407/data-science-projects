# Understanding the Role of a Data Scientist

## Learning Objectives
After watching this video, you will be able to:
- Summarize the role and tasks of data scientists.
- Identify some of the main skills a data scientist needs.
- Describe some possible career paths for data scientists.

---

## Data Roles in the Ecosystem (Start: 0:48)

In the data ecosystem, job titles and responsibilities often overlap. Here's a comparison of common roles:

### **Data Analyst**
- Translates data into plain language for decision-making.
- Identifies correlations, patterns, and applies statistical methods.
- Visualizes data to interpret and present findings.

### **Data Scientist**
- Analyzes data for actionable insights.
- Builds machine learning and deep learning models for predictive analysis.

### **Data Engineer**
- Extracts, cleans, organizes, and stores data.
- Prepares data repositories for use by other data professionals.

### **Other Roles**
- **Business Analysts / BI Analysts**: Make data-driven business decisions.
- **Collaborative Workflow**: All roles work together to extract, transform, and use data for predictions and insights with AI and ML.

---

## What Does a Data Scientist Do? (Start: 1:57)

Data scientists:
- Work with large volumes of structured and unstructured data.
- Clean and transform data for machine learning and AI model use.
- Design and implement ML/AI models to solve business problems.
- Combine statistics, business logic, and programming.
- Help with planning and decision-making using data from multiple sources.

---

## Real-World Business Questions Solved by Data Scientists (Start: 2:49)

Examples of business questions addressed:
- How many new social media followers will I gain next month?
- What percentage of my customers might I lose next quarter?
- Is this financial transaction unusual for this customer?

## Skills Required for Data Scientists

### **Technical Knowledge**
- Strong foundation in **statistics** and **probability**.
- Machine learning and cloud computing.
- (Optional but useful) Higher math like **calculus** and **linear algebra**.
- Programming (Python, SQL, R, C++, Java).
- Knowledge of **databases** and **data modeling**.

### **Tools & Technologies**
- **Python**: 86.7% of data scientists use Python at work; 79.9% recommend learning it first (Kaggle Survey).
- **Big Data**: Hadoop, Spark.
- **Visualization & BI Tools**: Power BI, Tableau.

---

## Soft Skills for Data Scientists (Start: 4:34)

- Communication & presentation skills.
- Critical and creative thinking.
- Storytelling with data.
- Collaboration with other data professionals.
- Diligence and perseverance.

---

## Career Paths for Data Scientists (Start: 5:39)

You can grow in various directions:
- Specialize in ML, deep learning, or advanced data analysis.
- Advance technical programming skills.
- Become a manager or team lead.
- Start your own consultancy.
- Leverage your **past experience or hobbies** to bring a unique perspective.

---

## Key Takeaways (Start: 6:44)

- Data scientists use their skills to identify patterns and make predictions from data.
- A career in data science requires specialized technical and domain knowledge.
- There are many possible career growth paths—technical, managerial, or entrepreneurial.
- Data science is one of the **fastest-growing professions** globally.
