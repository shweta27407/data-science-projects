# Expert Viewpoints: Standing Out from the Crowd

In this video, experienced data science professionals share what sets great candidates apart in a competitive job market.

---

## 🎯 Key Qualities of a Great Data Science Candidate

### 📚 1. Strong Technical Foundations

#### ✅ Core Mathematical Knowledge
- Solid understanding of **machine learning** and **deep learning** concepts.
- You don’t need to master advanced math like differential equations, but:
  - Know **how algorithms work under the hood** (especially linear algebra, statistics, optimization).
  - **Recommended Courses**:
    - *Machine Learning Specialization* – Coursera
    - *Introduction to Deep Learning* – DeepLearning.AI (builds models from scratch using NumPy)

#### ✅ Programming & Tools
- **Python** is essential (used across ML, AI, data wrangling, and visualization).
- **SQL** is crucial for data acquisition and transformation.
- **Shell scripting** is helpful in data pipeline automation.

#### ✅ Big Data & ML Libraries
- Familiarity with:
  - **Apache Spark** (especially Spark SQL and spark.ml)
  - **Scikit-learn**
  - **Keras/TensorFlow** for deep learning

---

## 🧠 2. Broader Data Science Skillset

### 🧩 Data Handling & Cloud Platforms
- Experience with **data lakes** and **data warehouses** in the cloud.
- Understanding how to **manage data in cloud environments** is becoming critical.

### 📊 Data Visualization & BI Tools
- Proficiency in tools like:
  - **Excel**
  - **Tableau**
  - **Power BI**
  - **Looker**
  - **MicroStrategy**

---

## 🧪 3. Research & Methodology

- Take **research methodology courses** in **mathematics** and **statistics**.
- Ability to:
  - Design and interpret experiments
  - Apply research techniques to practical problems

---

## 🧠 4. Critical Soft Skills

### 🗣️ Communication & Stakeholder Engagement
- Ability to **communicate complex ideas clearly** to non-technical stakeholders.
- Adjust communication based on domain (finance vs healthcare, etc.).
- Be strategic in presenting data solutions—**context and impact matter**.

### 💼 Business Analysis
- Understand business problems from a **domain-specific lens**.
- Know how to **translate data insights into business value**.

### 🔍 Research & Problem Solving
- Know how to **Google effectively**.
- Be resourceful in seeking documentation, blogs, community solutions.

### 💪 Grit & Resilience
- Data science projects will have **ups and downs**.
- The best candidates:
  - Push through failures.
  - Stay motivated during long debugging, modeling, or data prep phases.
