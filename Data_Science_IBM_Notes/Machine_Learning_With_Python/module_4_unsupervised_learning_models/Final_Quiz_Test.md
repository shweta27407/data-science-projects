# Module 4 Quiz Summary

## ✅ Question 1  
**A data scientist receives a large transaction log without any labels. What is the primary objective of applying an unsupervised algorithm to this data?**  
**✔️ Correct Answer:** *Discover hidden structures and similarities without labels*  
> Unsupervised learning reveals natural groupings or patterns that exist in raw, unlabeled data.

---

## ✅ Question 2  
**A marketing team wants to use agglomerative hierarchical clustering on delivery routes. Which of the following potential drawbacks should they look for in the large dataset?**  
**✔️ Correct Answer:** *Make the algorithm slow by repeating distance calculations*  
> Agglomerative clustering can be computationally expensive on large datasets due to repeated merging and distance computations.

---

## ✅ Question 3  
**A video streaming service uses K-means for recommending personalized content to its users based on their viewing history. Which of the following attributes would be the most relevant for clustering users?**  
**✔️ Correct Answer:** *The set of genres watched most frequently by each user*  
> K-means works best when features directly relate to the behavior being clustered, like genre preferences for content recommendations.

---

## ✅ Question 4  
**A bank wants to detect fraudulent transactions by analyzing customer spending patterns using DBSCAN. Why is DBSCAN a good fit for this analysis?**  
**✔️ Correct Answer:** *It groups data based on density and can isolate suspicious events*  
> DBSCAN helps identify dense spending clusters and outlier transactions, which can signal fraud.

---

## ✅ Question 5  
**Why does a genomics lab leverage t-SNE to plot gene-expression samples?**  
**✔️ Correct Answer:** *Highlights clear local patterns among similar samples in 2-D*  
> t-SNE focuses on preserving local structure, making biological sub-clusters visible.

---

## ✅ Question 6  
**A marketing team wants to analyze customer purchasing behavior using multiple features. They use PCA to reduce dimensionality. What advantages does PCA offer over other techniques?**  
**✔️ Correct Answer:** *Transforms features to capture data variance*  
> PCA reduces feature space while retaining most variance, simplifying analysis without major loss of information.

---

## ✅ Question 7  
**A research team visualizes clustering patterns of gene expression data using t-SNE. Why is t-SNE a good choice?**  
**✔️ Correct Answer:** *It preserves local relationships between similar data points*  
> t-SNE is ideal for visualizing local clustering in high-dimensional data.
