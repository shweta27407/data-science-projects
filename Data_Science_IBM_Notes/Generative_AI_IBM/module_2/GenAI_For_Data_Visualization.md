  - ✅ Bar chart of male vs. female students  
  - ✅ Pie chart of average weekly alcohol consumption (`Walc`) by gender
- Features:
  - Smart titles and AI insights

  - Export as PNG, SVG, CSV, JSON

### 2. **Akkio (Free Trial)** – No-code AI for data insights

  - ✅ **Scatter plot**: Marketing spend vs. Sales

## 📌 Key Visual Insights

| Chart Type        | Purpose                                      |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Bar Chart         | Compare categorical counts (e.g., Gender)    |
| Pie Chart         | Proportional comparison (e.g., `Walc` by gender) |
| Scatter Plot      | Relationship between 2 numerical variables   |
| Correlation Matrix| Strength & direction of attribute relationships |
| Box Plot          | Identify outliers                            |
| Histogram         | Understand distribution of single variable   |
