# 🎯 Skills for Data Professionals to Leverage Generative AI

Welcome to this expert viewpoints video where seasoned professionals share the **essential skills** required to effectively integrate **Generative AI (GenAI)** into data science workflows and projects.

---

## 🧠 1. Foundational Programming & Tools

- **Learn Python** — the most widely used language in AI and Data Science.
- **Familiarity with Libraries**:
  - `TensorFlow`, `PyTorch`, `Keras` – for ML/DL model development.
  - `Hugging Face Transformers` – for GenAI and NLP workflows.
  - `Scikit-learn`, `Matplotlib`, `Plotly`, `Seaborn` – for data manipulation and visualization.

> 💡 **Quote**: *“Python is the best for most of the AI and data science related frameworks.”*

---

## 🧩 2. Understand Generative AI Architectures

- Key model types:
  - **Transformers**
  - **Encoder / Decoder models**
  - **Embeddings**
  - **Diffusion models**
  - **GANs**
- Study **attention mechanisms** and **NLP foundations**.

---

## 🗣️ 3. Master Prompt Engineering

- Learn how to craft **effective prompts** for various LLMs.
- Use techniques like:
  - **Few-shot prompting**
  - **Chain of thought**
  - **Memory-enabled prompts**
- Incorporate **LangChain** for complex workflows.

> 💬 “With GenAI, it’s not about doing — it’s about getting your work done.”

---

## 📚 4. Deepen ML & DL Knowledge

- Understand core ML concepts:
  - **Regression**, **Classification**, **Clustering**
- Study **RNNs**, **CNNs**, and their evolution into GenAI models.
- Learn **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** and **agentic workflows**.

---

## 🧮 5. Mathematics & Theory

- Gain knowledge of:
  - **Linear Algebra**
  - **Matrix Computations**
  - **Probability and Statistics**

---

## 🧪 6. Data Manipulation & Model Evaluation

- Work with **real-world datasets**
- Skills in:
  - **Data cleaning & preprocessing**
  - **Evaluating AI-generated outputs**
  - **Understanding strengths/limitations** of GenAI outputs

> ✅ “Data professionals should understand how GenAI works, what kind of data it’s trained on, and where it should or should not be used.”

---

## 🎨 7. Data Visualization

- Tools to learn:
  - `Matplotlib`
  - `Seaborn`
  - `Plotly`
  - `Tableau` / `Power BI` (optional)
- Helps interpret model output effectively.

---

## ☁️ 8. MLOps & Deployment

- Learn how to **deploy AI models** using:
  - **Docker**
  - **Kubernetes**
  - **Cloud platforms** (AWS, Azure, GCP)

---

## 🔍 9. Evaluation & Iteration

- Learn to **quickly evaluate** GenAI outputs.
- Iterate fast to refine models, prompts, and applications.

---

## 🧘 10. Soft Skills

- **Problem-solving**
- **Adaptability**
- **Ethical decision-making**
- **Communication** — especially around AI usage and limitations.

---

## ✅ Final Advice

> “Remember, GenAI is a tool. Your ability to prompt it, guide it, and evaluate its output defines your success as a data professional.”
