# 📘 Course Syllabus and Prerequisites

## 🧠 Abstract

The landscape of **data science** is undergoing a dramatic transformation, fueled by the rise of a powerful new tool: **generative AI**. This cutting-edge technology transcends the traditional role of data analysis, venturing beyond extracting insights from existing data into the realm of *creation*.

**Generative AI** empowers data scientists to generate entirely new data, unlocking a universe of possibilities and tackling previously insurmountable challenges.

This course explores **generative AI tools** and their applications in data science and aims to empower data professionals with cutting-edge skills and knowledge. 

You will:
- Explore generative AI applications
- Engage in hands-on use
- Consider ethical implications in data science

By the end, you will know how to integrate generative AI into data science workflows, including data augmentation and feature engineering.

> ✅ **Note:** Free versions/trials of generative AI tools are used in this course. Paid versions are **not required**. Any optional upgrades are the learner’s responsibility.

---

## 🎯 Course Learning Objectives

After completing this course, you will be able to:

- Explain fundamental concepts of generative AI and its importance in data science.
- Apply generative AI techniques to real-world problems: data augmentation, generation, and feature engineering.
- Analyze real-world case studies where generative AI was used successfully.
- Discuss advantages, challenges, and ethical implications of generative AI in data workflows.
- Gain hands-on experience by building generative AI models in labs and projects.
- Apply concepts in a complete data science project involving generative AI.
- Communicate effectively through discussions and forums on generative AI and data science.
